About

CavBot gives teams one clear place to understand website health after launch. It watches the signals that affect trust, recovery, SEO structure, and user experience so teams can respond
with context instead of guesswork.

Built for founders, operators, and digital teams, CavBot turns post-launch website intelligence
into practical direction: what happened, why it matters, and what should happen next.

Services

I designed the structure, navigation, dashboards, and everyday flows across CavBot.

I built the front end and back end, including accounts, projects, authentication, APIs, databases, and the systems that connect the product.

I connected multiple AI models to CavBot and built the tools around them for writing, coding, website review, and product assistance.

I built the search foundations across CavBot, including page metadata, structured data, indexing rules, canonical URLs, and technical SEO.

I shaped CavBot’s visual identity, logo system, color direction, typography, and product language.

Process


CavBot started with a simple idea: make broken pages more useful. As I kept building, I saw a much bigger problem. Website owners were jumping between different tools just to understand what was happening on their site. I expanded CavBot around that problem, designing and building one product for finding issues, understanding them, and keeping a website healthy after launch.

Results


Built and launched CavBot as a complete software product, including the public website, accounts and projects, website monitoring, reporting, 404 recovery, AI tools, and the systems that support the product behind the scenes.
