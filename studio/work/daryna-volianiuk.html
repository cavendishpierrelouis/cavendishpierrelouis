About

Daryna Volianiuk is an AI researcher and cognitive scientist studying how people think,
learn, use language, and make decisions.

Her research focuses on human reasoning, artificial intelligence, and the ways people
understand and respond to intelligent systems. The portfolio brings together her
current research, projects, publications, education, and writing in English, Ukrainian,
and Polish.

Services

I designed Daryna Volianiuk’s logo mark, wordmark, typography, colors, and the visual system used across the portfolio.

I built the portfolio from front end to site functionality, including navigation, responsive layouts, language switching, and full support for English, Ukrainian, and Polish.

I set up the metadata, structured data, canonical URLs, indexing rules, and search foundations across the site.

Process


Daryna’s portfolio started with the research itself. I organized the site around what she studies, what she has built, and what she has published, then shaped the design around that content so nothing competes with it. I also built the identity, multilingual experience, and supporting site structure so the portfolio feels consistent from the first page to the last.

Results


Designed and built Daryna Volianiuk’s complete research portfolio, including the logo identity, research, projects, publications, education, profile, contact, responsive layouts, English, Ukrainian, and Polish support, and the search foundations behind the site.
