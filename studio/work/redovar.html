About

Redovar helps staff get through the day without relying on personal phones.

Staff can follow schedules, receive updates, communicate, ask for help, and stay informed throughout the day without relying on personal phones.

Camps and businesses can organize Redovar around the way they already work, while one shared system keeps everything connected behind the scenes.

Services

I designed the structure, flows, and product experience across Redovar.

I built the Redovar Console, and the Staff Device app, including sign-in, databases, live updates, and device management.

I built the search foundations across Redovar, including metadata, structured data, indexing rules, canonical URLs, and technical SEO.

I shaped Redovar’s visual identity, logo system, color direction, typography, and product language.

Process


My cofounder and I had both worked at camp and seen the same problem up close. Personal phones were being used for schedules and communication, but they also brought distractions into work that required people to stay present. Redovar started from that experience.

Results


I designed and built Redovar around a dedicated staff device and a web-based Console. I also built the internal Ops system used to manage the company, along with the account system and the infrastructure behind the product.
