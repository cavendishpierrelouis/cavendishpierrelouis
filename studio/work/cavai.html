About

The intelligence layer inside CavBot. CavAi helps people understand problems across their
website without digging through different tools or reports.

It explains problems in plain language, highlights what matters, and helps teams decide what
to fix next.

Services

I designed the structure, navigation, conversation flow, and everyday interactions across CavAi.

I built the front end and back end that power conversations, follow-ups, saved history, and the connection between
CavAi and CavBot.

I built the AI system that lets CavAi use information from the website and project someone is reviewing, explain problems clearly, and suggest the next step.

I shaped CavAi’s visual identity, logo system, color direction, typography, and product language.

Process


CavAi started with a simple idea: CavBot should be able to explain a website problem, not just show it. Instead of building a separate chatbot, I built CavAi directly into CavBot so it can use the website and project someone is already looking at. I focused on making each conversation clear, useful, and tied to the problem in front of them.

Results


Built and launched CavAi inside CavBot, with conversations, follow-up questions, saved history, and answers shaped around the website
someone is reviewing.
